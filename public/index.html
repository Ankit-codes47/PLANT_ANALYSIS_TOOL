About

About PlantScan

PlantScan is an AI-powered plant analysis interface designed to help users understand plants from photographs and receive useful care guidance.

It provides AI-generated insights for informational purposes and can help interpret visible plant characteristics, but results may vary depending on image quality and conditions.

  • AI-powered image analysis
  • Plant health and care guidance
  • Clear, accessible insights
  • Downloadable PDF reports

Built for curious plant owners

Whether you are growing houseplants, learning about species, or checking a plant’s condition, PlantScan helps turn a simple photo into useful, practical understanding.
